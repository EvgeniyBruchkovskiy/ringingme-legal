Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RingingMe (“we”, “us”, the “App”) handles your information. In short: RingingMe works on your iPhone. We do not run accounts, we do not sell data, and your conversations, diary entries and recordings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Voice and speech. During calls the App uses the microphone to hear your answers. Speech is recognized on your device by Apple’s speech framework. Recordings of evening check-ins are saved only in the App’s storage on your iPhone.
  • Diary. Transcripts, day summaries and mood notes are generated and stored on your device. Summaries are created with Apple’s on-device model (Apple Intelligence) where available.
  • Alarms and settings. Alarm times, characters and preferences are stored on your device.
  • Motion. If a wake-up call asks you to walk, the App reads your step count from the device’s motion sensor for that call only.
  • Plan4U agenda (optional). If you turn on Plan4U sync in Settings and the Plan4U app is installed, RingingMe reads today’s agenda from a shared on-device container so your character can mention your plans. This data is not sent anywhere.
  • Clips. Video clips of calls are rendered on your device and are saved to Photos or shared only when you choose to.

What we collect

The current version of the App does not send your personal data to our servers and does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.

If we introduce optional cloud features in the future (for example, cloud voices or anonymous usage statistics that you opt in to), we will update this policy and the App’s privacy details on the App Store before enabling them.

Purchases

Subscriptions and seasonal packs are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details; we only receive confirmation from Apple of which products you are entitled to.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete all App data at any time in Settings → Privacy → Delete all data. Deleting the App also removes its data from your device. Data included in your iCloud or device backups is governed by Apple’s backup settings.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
