FAQ

The character didn’t call me.

Make sure you allowed alarms for RingingMe (Settings → RingingMe → Alarms) and that the alarm is switched on in the App. Alarms ring even in Silent mode and Focus.

The character can’t hear me.

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for RingingMe in iOS Settings. Speak after the character finishes its line.

I hung up and went back to sleep.

That’s expected to fail: if you end the call before the character lets you go, it calls back in 3 minutes.

How do I restore purchases or cancel my subscription?

Tap “Restore purchases” on the Pro screen. To cancel, open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RingingMe.

How do I delete my data?

In the App: Settings → Privacy → Delete all data. Everything is stored only on your iPhone.
